Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Store & Apple App Store Data Safety Policies

1. Overview

At Yaalu, we believe you should always maintain full sovereignty over your personal data. You have the unconditional right to request the permanent deletion of your Yaalu account and all associated personal data at any time.

Below, you will find instructions for deleting your account directly inside the app, or submitting a remote deletion request if you have already uninstalled the application.

2. Method A: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Yaalu app installed on your smartphone, you can instantly erase your account within seconds:

Step 1: Open Settings

Launch the Yaalu app and tap on your Profile tab at the bottom right, then tap the Settings (Gear) icon.

Step 2: Access Account Settings

Scroll down to the Account Management section and tap on Delete Account.

Step 3: Confirm Deletion

Confirm your action. Your session will be immediately invalidated, your profile removed from discovery, and your data scheduled for permanent erasure.

3. Method B: Remote Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app, lost access to your device, or cannot log in, you can request manual deletion by sending an email to our Data Privacy & Safety Team:

Submit Deletion Request via Email

Send an email from your contact address or include your registered phone number (with country code, e.g. +9471XXXXXXX) so we can locate and purge your account records.

✉️ Email support@yaalu.lk to Delete Account

Note: Remote requests are verified and processed within 24 to 48 business hours. You will receive a confirmation once deletion is complete.

4. Data Deletion Scope & Retention Details

When your account is deleted, here is what happens to your data:

🗑️ Permanently Deleted Immediately

  • Profile details (Name, bio, gender, age, interests)
  • All uploaded profile photos and selfie verification pictures
  • All recorded voice notes and audio clips
  • All direct messages, matches, likes, and swipe history
  • GPS location history and device tokens
  • Phone number records from active user pools

⏳ Limited Retention (When Applicable)

  • Financial transaction records (if coins or VIP passes were purchased via Google Play / Apple) are retained up to statutory financial auditing requirements (e.g., tax compliance).
  • Anonymized abuse & ban prevention hashes (e.g. SHA-256 hash of a banned phone number) may be retained strictly to prevent banned predatory users from returning.
