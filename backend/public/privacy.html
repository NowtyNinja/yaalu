Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 • Version 1.0
Your Privacy Matters: Yaalu ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data when using the Yaalu mobile application and services.

1. Age Requirement & Eligibility (Strictly 18+)

Yaalu is exclusively created for adults aged 18 years or older. We strictly prohibit minors from creating an account or accessing our services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we discover that a user is underage, their account and all associated profile data will be permanently deleted immediately.

2. Information We Collect

To provide matching and connection services, we collect information that you directly provide to us, as well as automatic technical information:

  • Account & Phone Verification: Your mobile phone number is collected to securely verify your identity via SMS One-Time Password (OTP) codes powered by telecom SMS gateways (e.g., Notify.lk).
  • Profile Information: Your display name, date of birth / age, gender, sexual orientation, dating preferences, biography, interests, and relationship goals.
  • User-Generated Media: Profile photos, selfie verification images, and voice recordings / audio notes that you choose to upload or record within the app.
  • Location Data: With your explicit device permission, we collect your approximate or precise geographic location (GPS) to show you potential matches in your vicinity. You can enable or disable location services at any time via your device settings.
  • Messages & Interactions: We store your matches, likes, passes, and chat messages (including text and voice notes) to facilitate real-time communication between matched users.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: IP address, device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash reports to ensure security and prevent fraudulent accounts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect for the following core purposes:

  • To create and administer your Yaalu account and verify phone authenticity.
  • To provide nearby match suggestions and enable swiping, matching, and in-app chat.
  • To verify profile authenticity and curb catfishing or spam bots.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent prohibited activities, harassment, hate speech, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To provide customer support, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve app performance.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We NEVER sell your personal data or private messages to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

We only share data in the following restricted scenarios:

  • Other Yaalu Users: Your public profile information (photos, name, age, bio, interests, and distance) is visible to other users for matchmaking. Your phone number is strictly private and NEVER shown to other users.
  • Service Providers: We work with trusted infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, SMS gateways such as Notify.lk for OTP delivery) strictly bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal & Safety Obligations: We may disclose data if required by applicable laws (including the laws of Sri Lanka, Computer Crimes Act No. 24 of 2007) or in response to a valid court order or emergency situations threatening personal safety.

5. Security of Your Data

We employ robust industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All client-to-server data transmission is secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) and encrypted WebSockets (WSS).
  • Data access is restricted to authorized personnel with multi-factor authentication.
  • Passwords and sensitive tokens are hashed using cryptographic salt functions.

6. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal information:

  • Review & Edit: You can edit your photos, bio, interests, and preferences directly in your profile settings.
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time within the app by navigating to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently wipes your profile, photos, matches, and chat history.
  • Web-Based Account Deletion: In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, you can also request account and data deletion via our web portal at /delete-account without needing the app installed.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates occur, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of Yaalu after any modifications indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
